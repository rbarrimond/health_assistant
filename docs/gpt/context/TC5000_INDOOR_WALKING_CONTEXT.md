# TC5000 Indoor Walking Context Document

**Bowflex® TreadClimber® 5000 -- Primary Indoor Walking Modality:**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Machine Identity

- **Model:** Bowflex® TreadClimber® TC5000
- **Primary Mode Used:** TreadClimber Mode (belts + hydraulic stepping engaged)
- **User Recording Method:** Apple Watch → Apple Workout → *Indoor Walk*
- **Heart Rate Source:** Apple Watch (chest strap not in use)
- **Session Structure:** Ad hoc --- performed when systemically optimal

This document defines how TC5000 sessions are interpreted and integrated into long‑term aerobic durability training.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Mechanical Characteristics (Training-Relevant)

### Speed Range

- 0.7 -- 4.0 mph

Intensity is determined by:

- Belt speed
- Hydraulic resistance level (Workout Level 1--12)
- Step depth (treadle displacement)

### Resistance System

- Dual hydraulic cylinders
- Workout Level dial: 1--12
  - 1 = minimal displacement
  - 12 = maximal displacement
- Ideal displacement: \~3 inches
- "Bottoming out" indicates excessive travel

**Training meaning:**

- Belt speed primarily drives metabolic cost
- Hydraulic level primarily drives muscular demand
- Combined effect behaves closer to uphill hiking than flat treadmill walking

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Console Metrics (TC5000)

Available machine metrics:

- Calories / Calories per minute
- Time
- Miles
- Total steps
- Speed (MPH)
- Climb indicator (200 steps = 100 ft vertical gain)

Important: Calorie accuracy depends on:

- Correct weight entry
- Correct Workout Level input

If these are not entered accurately, calorie values are considered secondary data.

Primary physiological data source = **Apple Watch heart rate**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Data Hierarchy for Interpretation

### Primary Signals

1. Duration
2. Apple Watch heart rate
3. Average speed
4. Workout Level
5. Subjective breathing (nasal vs mouth)

### Secondary Signals

- Calories (only if weight/level correctly entered)
- Step count
- Climb indicator

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Aerobic Classification Model

### Recovery Session

- Level 1--4
- Low speed
- HR in low Z1
- Nasal breathing fully sustainable
- No next-day fatigue

### Aerobic Builder (Zone 2 Equivalent)

- HR in Z1--Z2 range
- Nasal breathing mostly sustainable
- Stable HR (minimal upward drift)
- 30--75 minutes

Primary durability builder.

### Muscular Endurance Builder

- Level 7--12
- Moderate speed
- HR moderate Z2
- Noticeable glute/quad loading
- Mild HR drift acceptable

Structural aerobic + muscular hybrid stimulus.

### Intensity Replacement (Rare Use)

- High speed + high level
- Sustained mouth breathing
- HR near threshold
- ≥20 minutes sustained effort

Used cautiously due to cycling integration priorities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Integration Into Cycling System

Primary goal: Build aerobic durability and long-term cycling capacity.

TC5000 walking is treated as: - Low joint stress aerobic volume - Optional cycling replacement on poor-weather days - Systemic load modifier

Decision logic: - After high-intensity cycling → only Recovery or low Aerobic Builder walk - On base days → Aerobic Builder walk acceptable - During fatigue accumulation → Recovery walk preferred - Never stack high TC5000 intensity with cycling intensity

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Operational Constraints That Affect Training

- Hydraulic level must not be changed during movement
- Belts start at 0.7 mph
- Mode change requires power-off
- Maintenance (lubrication) affects friction and HR drift

If HR trends upward abnormally at fixed speed: - Check lubrication schedule - Inspect belt tension - Inspect alignment

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Maintenance Awareness

Lubrication schedule guideline: - \<3 hrs/week → every 6 months - 3--5 hrs/week → every 3 months - \>5 hrs/week → monthly

Increased friction can: - Raise heart rate artificially - Increase muscular fatigue - Reduce data consistency

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Practical Reporting Format for Sessions

To allow precise interpretation, sessions should report:

- Duration
- Average speed
- Workout Level
- Average HR
- Peak HR
- Breathing notes (nasal / mixed / mouth)
- Next-day fatigue (yes/no)

Example:

> 45 min\
> 3.2 mph\
> Level 6\
> Avg HR 124 bpm\
> Nasal most of session\
> Felt normal next day

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. System Positioning Summary

TC5000 walking is: - Not casual walking - Not flat treadmill equivalent - Not random cardio

It is a controllable, repeatable, vertically-loaded aerobic stimulus that behaves closer to structured incline hiking.

Within the durability system, it functions as: - Aerobic volume builder - Structural reinforcement tool - Low-impact conditioning bridge between cycling days
